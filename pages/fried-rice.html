Fried Rice

This Easy Fried Rice has always been one of the most-loved recipes on my site! I have made different versions, but this classic recipe is the one everyone keeps coming back to because it's so easy and delicious!

Ingredients

  • 3 cups of cooked rice
  • 2 tablespoons sesame oil
  • 1 cup chopped white onion 1 small onion
  • 1 cup of frozen peas and carrots
  • 2 lightly beaten eggs

Instructions

  1. Preheat a large skillet or wok to medium heat. Add 2 tablespoons sesame oil, 1 cup chopped white onion, and 1 cup frozen peas and carrots. Cook until tender.
  2. Slide the vegetables to the side and pour 2 lightly beaten eggs onto the other side of the skillet. Using a spatula, scramble the eggs. Once cooked, mix the eggs with the vegetables.
  3. Add 3 cups cooked rice * to the veggie and egg mixture. Pour 2-3 tablespoons soy sauce on top. Stir the rice and veggie mixture until warmed through and combined.
  4. Top with 2 tablespoons chopped green onions, if desired, and serve immediately.
